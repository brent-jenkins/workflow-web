A list shows everything. Zayvori shows what’s next.

Real projects aren’t a straight line. Zayvori keeps back what can’t be done yet, so everything in front of you is something you can actually do. Here’s the same kitchen remodel both ways.

A usual to-do list

  • Get a bid from contractor 1
  • Get a bid from contractor 2
  • Hire a contractor
  • Order countertops
  • Schedule the electrician
  • Tear out the old kitchen
  • Install cabinets

Seven things, all asking for attention. Which can you do today? You have to work it out again every time you look.

Zayvori

Two things you can do now. The rest waits its turn and says what it’s waiting for. When the first bid comes back, the other is set aside for you.

See the whole job. Then just do the next bit.

Every plan has a map. Green is done, the highlighted tasks are ready, and anything you no longer need quietly steps aside.

  • Branch and merge. Run jobs side by side and bring them back together.
  • Finish all, or any one. Choose whether a step waits for everything before it, or just the first.
  • Build as you think. Add a task after another, one alongside, or slot one in between. It lays itself out.

On a tablet, see it all at once

Turn it sideways and your plans, the next steps and the whole map sit side by side. Check something off and the map moves on with you.

Everything for today, from every plan

A move, a kitchen remodel and a birthday party all at once? The Today tab gathers what’s overdue, what’s due today and your next steps, across all of them. Check it off right there.

Running late? Check off a task a few days behind and Zayvori offers to move what comes after it, so nothing ends up due too soon.

Just say what you’re planning

Type it, or tap the mic and say it: “Create a study plan for the AP Biology exam on May 10, starting January 4, covering the College Board’s course units. Keep doing past free-response questions alongside the units, and review the labs too. Include links to past exam questions and any other useful resources.”

AI drafts the steps, what waits on what, and when each is due: the eight units in order through the spring, with free-response practice and the labs running alongside, and all three meeting at a timed practice exam two weeks out. The College Board, Khan Academy and Bozeman Science pages are listed in the notes, one per line, ready to tap.

It plans by the hour as well as the day. Try “Christmas dinner for 10, everything ready by 3pm” and every dish gets its own timed steps, from the turkey going in to carving, with handy notes like oven temperatures. Holidays such as Thanksgiving and Mother’s Day land on your country’s date.

The draft opens on the map, ready to change. Nothing starts until you tap Begin.

And the little things that make it work

Reminders you can act on
A heads-up before something’s due, another when it is, and a morning summary. Mark it complete or snooze it for an hour, right from the notification.
Checklists inside a task
“Pack the kitchen” doesn’t need five tasks of its own. Plates, glasses and pans go in a checklist, and the task shows 2/5 as you go.
Notes you can tap
The plumber’s number, a product link, the school’s email: one tap calls, opens or writes.
Plans that bend when things slip
Move a date and Zayvori offers to move whatever now comes too soon, keeping the gaps between them.
Plans that come around again
Monthly bills, the quarterly tax return, yearly car maintenance: set it to repeat and the next round starts on schedule.
Your next steps, on your home screen
The Next steps widget lists what you can do now, overdue first. Check one off without opening the app.
Undo, always
Checked something off by mistake? Undo is right there. Finish the last task and there’s confetti.
Light or dark, your colors
Follows your phone, or pick your own. Each plan gets its own colors, so you can spot it at a glance.

Your plans stay on your phone

There’s no account to make and no server holding your tasks. Zayvori keeps everything on your device, so it works anywhere and nobody else sees your plans. (The AI features in Premium are the exception: when you use Describe it or Change with AI, what you type, and for Change with AI that one plan, is sent to draft the changes. Nothing else is.) Back up to a file whenever you like and keep it in Drive, your email or your files, ready for a new phone.
