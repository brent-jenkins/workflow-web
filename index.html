Everything for today, from every plan

A move, a kitchen remodel and a birthday party all at once? The Today tab gathers what’s overdue, what’s due today and what you can do next, across all of them. Check it off right there.

Just say what you’re planning

Type it, or tap the mic and say it: “Garden party on Saturday. Invite friends, buy the food and drinks, tidy the garden and set up tables.” AI drafts the steps, what waits on what, and when each is due.

It plans by the hour as well as the day. Try “Christmas dinner for 10, everything ready by 3pm” and every dish gets its own timed steps, from the turkey going in to carving, with handy notes like oven temperatures. Holidays such as Thanksgiving and Mother’s Day land on your country’s date.

It’s good for exam prep, too. “AP Bio exam on May 11. Go through every unit, do the past free-response questions, two full practice tests” becomes weeks of steps that build toward the day, with notes on where to find the material.

The draft opens on the map, ready to change. Nothing starts until you tap Begin.

And the little things that make it work

Reminders you can act on
A heads-up before something’s due, another when it is, and a morning summary. Mark it complete or snooze it for an hour, right from the notification.
Checklists inside a task
“Pack the kitchen” doesn’t need five tasks of its own. Plates, glasses and pans go in a checklist, and the task shows 2/5 as you go.
Notes you can tap
The plumber’s number, a product link, the school’s email: one tap calls, opens or writes.
Plans that bend when things slip
Move a date and Zayvori offers to move whatever now comes too soon, keeping the gaps between them.
Plans that come around again
Monthly bills, the quarterly tax return, yearly car maintenance: set it to repeat and the next round starts on schedule.
What’s next, on your home screen
The Ready now widget lists what you can do next, overdue first. Check one off without opening the app.
Undo, always
Checked something off by mistake? Undo is right there. Finish the last task and there’s confetti.
Light or dark, your colors
Follows your phone, or pick your own. Each plan gets its own colors, so you can spot it at a glance.

Your plans stay on your phone

There’s no account to make and no server holding your tasks. Zayvori keeps everything on your device, so it works anywhere and nobody else sees your plans. (The AI features in Premium are the exception: when you use Describe it or Change with AI, what you type, and for Change with AI that one plan, is sent to draft the changes. Nothing else is.) Back up to a file whenever you like and keep it in Drive, your email or your files, ready for a new phone.
