Features

Plans have shapes. Your to-do list should too.

Real projects aren't a straight line. Some jobs wait on others, some happen at the same time, and some only matter if something else falls through.

Map it out

Lay tasks out as a flow from Start to Finish. Branch into jobs that run side by side, then bring them back together.

Always know what's next

The To do view shows only what's ready right now, with what's coming up underneath. One tap ticks it off.

Decide by doing

Chasing two quotes? Set the next step to “any one is enough” and the first to come back moves you on. The other is set aside for you.

Reminders that help

A nudge before something's due, another when it is, and a morning summary. Mark it complete right from the notification.

Progress you can see

“3 of 6 tasks completed” and a bar that fills as you go. Finish the last one and the confetti's on us.

Private by design

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no sign-up, nothing sent to a server. It works without a signal.

The map

See the whole job. Then just do the next bit.

Every workflow has a map. Green is done, the glowing task is ready, and anything you no longer need quietly steps aside.

  • Branch and merge. Run jobs side by side and join them back up.
  • Finish all, or any one. Choose whether a step waits for everything before it, or just the first.
  • Build as you think. Add the next task, one alongside, or slot one in between. It lays itself out.
How it works

From plan to done in three steps

Plan it

Name a workflow and add tasks. Add the next one, one alongside, or connect to something you've already got. Give anything a due date.

Begin

Tap Begin and Zayvori works out what's ready. Your To do view fills with just the things you can do right now.

Tick it off

Tick tasks as you go, in the app or from a reminder. What's next unlocks by itself, until the last tick finishes the job.

Private

Your plans stay on your phone

There's no account to make and no server holding your tasks. Zayvori keeps everything on your device, so it works anywhere — and nobody else ever sees it.
