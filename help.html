How Zayvori works

A short guide to the parts worth knowing about.

Zayvori is a to-do app for jobs with more than one step. You lay a job out as a workflow: tasks in order, side by side, or coming back together. Then Zayvori shows you only what you can do next.

Getting started

Tap + Workflow and choose how to start:

  • Describe it. Type or say your plan in your own words, such as “Garden party on Saturday. Invite friends, buy the food and drinks, tidy the garden.” AI drafts the tasks, what waits on what, and when each is due. Check it over and change anything you like.
  • From a template. Pick a ready-made plan and a start date, and every due date falls into place.
  • Blank. Start with just Start and Finish, then add tasks.

A new workflow starts out in Planning, so you can arrange it without anything nagging you. When it looks right, tap Begin.

How a workflow works

Every task is in one of these states:

  • Ready: you can do it now.
  • Waiting: it’s waiting for tasks before it. Tap it to see which.
  • Done: you ticked it off.
  • Skipped: you set it aside yourself. You can bring it back with Restore.
  • Not needed: Zayvori set it aside for you, because another route already got the job done.

When a task has more than one task leading into it, you choose what it waits for:

  • Finish all of them: it’s ready once every task before it is done. Use this for things that all need doing, like ordering the countertops and booking the electrician before the cabinets go in.
  • Any one is enough: it’s ready as soon as one of them is done, and the others become “Not needed”. Use this for alternatives, like getting bids from two contractors and hiring whichever comes back first.

Changed your mind? Ticking a task off shows Undo at the bottom of the screen, and undoing also brings back anything it set aside.

Today, To do and the Map

  • Today (the first tab) brings together every workflow you’ve begun: what’s overdue, what’s due today, and everything else that’s ready now. Tick tasks off right there. You can choose which tab Zayvori opens on in Settings → Appearance → Start on.
  • To do, inside a workflow, shows that workflow’s ready tasks first, then what’s coming up and what each is waiting for, then what’s done.
  • The Map shows the whole workflow. Tap a task to open it, then use Next task, Task alongside or Connect to… to build. Tap a link between two tasks to insert a task there or remove the link. Pinch to zoom and drag to look around. If you drag tasks into your own arrangement, Tidy puts them back in order.

Due dates and reminders

Open a task and tap Add a due date. Zayvori reminds you before it’s due and again when it’s due, and can send a morning summary on days with something due or overdue. Choose how early, or turn reminders off, in Settings → Reminders.

Reminders for tasks you can do now have a Mark complete button, so you can tick a task off without opening the app.

When things slip. If you move a task’s date later, and that leaves the tasks after it due too soon, Zayvori offers to move them by the same amount. The same happens when you tick off a task a day or more late. Nothing moves unless you tap Move, and Undo puts the dates back. Tasks that still have time, like a fixed moving day, stay where they are.

Templates

A template is a plan you can use again. Its dates are relative, such as “Day 3, 9:00 AM”, so when you make a workflow from it and pick a start date, every due date falls into place.

  • Four templates come built in: Move to a new home, Kitchen remodel, Job application and Plan an event.
  • Save your own: open a workflow’s ⋯ menu and choose Save as template.
  • Send one to someone: choose Send (on a template) or Send as a template (on a workflow) and share it by message or email. Only the plan goes, not your progress. If they have Zayvori, they open the file with Zayvori, or use Open a template file… at the bottom of the Templates tab.

Repeating workflows

For jobs that come round again, like paying the bills, a tax return or a car service, open the workflow’s ⋯ menu and choose Repeat…: every week, every 2 weeks, every month, every 3 months or every year.

When you finish a round, the next one starts by itself, with its dates set from the schedule, and the finished round moves to Archived. The schedule stays fixed: if one round runs late, the next still starts on its usual date. A series that starts on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

You can also run any finished workflow again from its ⋯ menu with Run it again. The copy starts fresh, with its dates counted from today.

The home-screen widget

Add the Ready now widget from your phone’s widget list. It shows the tasks you can do next across all your workflows, overdue first. Tap a task’s circle to tick it off, or anywhere else to open Zayvori.

Backups and a new phone

Zayvori keeps everything on your phone, with no account. To keep a copy, go to Settings → Your data → Back up and save the file wherever you like, such as Google Drive or email.

To restore it, on the same phone or a new one, choose Restore and pick the file. Add to what’s here keeps what’s already on the phone and adds what’s missing. Replace everything swaps it all for the backup.
