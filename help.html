How Zayvori works

A short guide to the parts worth knowing about.

Zayvori is a to-do app for jobs with more than one step. You lay a job out as a plan: tasks in order, side by side, or coming back together. Then Zayvori shows you only your next steps: what you can do now.

Getting started

Tap + Plan and choose how to start:

  • Describe it. Type or say your plan in your own words, such as “Garden party on Saturday. Invite friends, buy the food and drinks, tidy the garden.” AI drafts the tasks, what waits on what, and when each is due. Give a time (“dinner ready by 3pm”) and it works back from it, step by step and hour by hour, with a short tip on each task. Check it over and change anything you like.
  • From a template. Pick a ready-made plan and a start date, and every due date falls into place.
  • Blank. Start with just Start and Finish, then add tasks.

A new plan starts out in Planning, so you can arrange it without anything nagging you. When it looks right, tap Begin.

How a plan works

Every task is in one of these states:

  • Ready: you can do it now.
  • Waiting: it’s waiting for tasks before it. Tap it to see which.
  • Done: you checked it off.
  • Skipped: you set it aside yourself. You can bring it back with Restore.
  • Not needed: Zayvori set it aside for you, because another route already got the job done.

When a task has more than one task leading into it, you choose what it waits for:

  • Finish all of them: it’s ready once every task before it is done. Use this for things that all need doing, like ordering the countertops and booking the electrician before the cabinets go in.
  • Any one is enough: it’s ready as soon as one of them is done, and the others become “Not needed”. Use this for alternatives, like getting bids from two contractors and hiring whichever comes back first.

Changed your mind? Checking a task off shows Undo at the bottom of the screen, and undoing also brings back anything it set aside.

Today, To do and the Map

  • Today (the first tab) brings together every plan you’ve begun: what’s overdue, what’s due today, and your other next steps. Check tasks off right there. You can choose which tab Zayvori opens on in Settings → Appearance → Start on.
  • To do, inside a plan, shows that plan’s ready tasks first, then what’s coming up and what each is waiting for, then what’s done.
  • The Map shows the whole plan. Tap a task to open it, then use Task after, Task alongside or Connect to… to build. Tap a link between two tasks to insert a task there or remove the link. Pinch to zoom and drag to look around. If you drag tasks into your own arrangement, Tidy puts them back in order.

Checklists and notes

For steps too small to be tasks of their own, open a task and add a checklist: “Pack the kitchen” might have plates, glasses and pans. Check items off as you go; the task shows how far you’ve got (2/5) on the Map, in To do and in Today. The task itself is still checked off by you, when you’re ready. Templates, copies and repeating plans keep the checklist, with every item unticked again.

In a task’s notes, web addresses, email addresses and phone numbers can be tapped to open them, so a plumber’s number or a product link is one tap away. Tap anywhere else in the notes, or the pencil, to edit them.

Due dates and reminders

Open a task and tap Add a due date. Zayvori reminds you before it’s due and again when it’s due, and can send a morning summary on days with something due or overdue. Choose how early, or turn reminders off, in Settings → Reminders.

Reminders for tasks you can do now have a Mark complete button, so you can check a task off without opening the app. Snooze 1 hour reminds you again an hour later, unless the task is done by then.

When things slip. If you move a task’s date later, and that leaves the tasks after it due too soon, Zayvori offers to move them by the same amount. The same happens when you check off a task a day or more late. Nothing moves unless you tap Move, and Undo puts the dates back. Tasks that still have time, like a fixed moving day, stay where they are.

Zayvori Premium

Describe it, Change with AI and Calendar sync are part of Zayvori Premium: $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year (local prices vary), with a 14-day free trial. Tap any of them, or Settings → Zayvori Premium, to start the trial. Everything else in Zayvori is free. You manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play; Settings → Zayvori Premium takes you straight there.

Changing a plan with AI

Plans change. Open a plan or template and tap the sparkle button at the top (or choose Change with AI from its ⋯ menu), then say what you’d like: “add selling the old car before moving day”, “push everything back a week”, or “split packing into one task per room”.

Zayvori shows you every suggested change first: what’s added, moved, renamed, reordered or removed. Nothing happens until you tap Apply, and Undo puts everything back exactly as it was. Tasks you’ve already done are never changed.

Templates

A template is a plan you can use again. Its dates are relative, such as “Day 3, 9:00 AM”, so when you make a plan from it and pick a start date, every due date falls into place.

  • Five templates come built in: Move to a new home, Kitchen remodel, Job application, Plan an event and Yearly car maintenance.
  • Save your own: open a plan’s ⋯ menu and choose Save as template.
  • Send one to someone: choose Send (on a template) or Send as a template (on a plan) and share it by message or email. Only the plan goes, not your progress. If they have Zayvori, they open the file with Zayvori, or use Open a template file… at the bottom of the Templates tab.

Repeating plans

For jobs that come around again, like paying the bills, a tax return or car maintenance, open the plan’s ⋯ menu and choose Repeat…: every week, every 2 weeks, every month, every 3 months or every year.

When you finish a round, the next one starts by itself, with its dates set from the schedule, and the finished round moves to Archived. The schedule stays fixed: if one round runs late, the next still starts on its usual date. A series that starts on the 31st falls on the last day of shorter months.

You can also run any finished plan again from its ⋯ menu with Run it again. The copy starts fresh, with its dates counted from today.

Calendar sync (Premium)

Turn on Settings → Calendar sync and choose a calendar: any on your phone that accepts new events, such as Google, Samsung or Outlook. For Outlook, first turn on Sync calendars in the Outlook app’s settings so its calendars show up.

Every task with a due date in a plan you’ve begun appears as a half-hour event at its due time, or as an all-day event if you prefer. Check a task off, change its date or delete it, and the calendar follows. To leave a plan out, choose Leave out of calendar from its ⋯ menu. Turning sync off removes Zayvori’s events.

The home-screen widget

Add the Next steps widget from your phone’s widget list. It shows your next steps across all your plans, overdue first. Tap a task’s circle to check it off, or anywhere else to open Zayvori.

Backups and a new phone

Zayvori keeps everything on your phone, with no account. To keep a copy, go to Settings → Your data → Back up and save the file wherever you like, such as Google Drive or email.

To restore it, on the same phone or a new one, choose Restore and pick the file. Add to what’s here keeps what’s already on the phone and adds what’s missing. Replace everything swaps it all for the backup.
