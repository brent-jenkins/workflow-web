Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

In short: your workflows, tasks and notes stay on your phone, and we never receive them. The one exception is Describe it: when you use it, the description you type is sent to Claude, an AI from Anthropic, to draft the steps, and nothing is kept afterwards. The website uses no cookies and no analytics.

Who we are

Zayvori is made by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, trading as Zayvori. There is no company behind it. That person is the data controller for the data described below, and can be reached at hello@zayvori.com.

The app

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you put in the app — workflows, tasks, notes, due dates and your settings — is stored only on your phone, and we never receive it.

  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. They are not sent from a server, and nothing about your tasks leaves the device to make them work. Pressing “Mark complete” on a reminder updates the app on your phone, and nowhere else.
  • Notification permission is only used to show those reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app under Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's settings.
  • Backups you make in Settings → Your data are files you save wherever you choose, such as Google Drive or email. We never receive them.
  • Android's own backup. Your phone may copy app data to your personal Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is yours: we cannot see or read it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Describe it

Describe it is optional, and nothing is sent until you choose to use it. The first time, the app tells you what is sent and asks you to continue. When you tap “Draft it”:

  • What is sent: the description you typed (or dictated with your keyboard), today's date on your phone, and a random identifier the app makes once when you first use the feature. Nothing else: not your other workflows, your name or your contacts.
  • Where it goes: to our service at api.zayvori.com, which runs on Cloudflare, and from there to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, which drafts the steps and sends them back to your phone.
  • What is kept: our service does not store or log your description. Cloudflare, like any network provider, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the service and keep it secure. Anthropic processes your description as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on it; Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its privacy policy.
  • The random identifier is used only to stop the service being overused. It isn't linked to you, your phone's hardware or anything else; uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Why: to provide the feature you asked for. Please don't include things you wouldn't want an AI service to process, such as health details or other people's personal information.

Cloudflare and Anthropic are based in the United States. Where your description is processed outside the UK or EU, it is protected by the safeguards those companies provide for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses. See Anthropic's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. We keep it only as long as we need to deal with your message and keep a record of it, and we never share it, sell it or add you to a mailing list. Our email provider stores messages on our behalf.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and nothing of yours is stored on our side. To delete a task or workflow, delete it in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. In practice that means any emails you have sent us; we hold nothing from Describe it. Email hello@zayvori.com and we will respond within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the authority where you live.

Children

Zayvori is not aimed at children. If a child has emailed us and a parent would like that email deleted, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
