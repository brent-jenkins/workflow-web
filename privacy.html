Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short: the app collects nothing. Your workflows, tasks and notes stay on your phone, and we never receive them. The website uses no cookies and no analytics. The only personal data we ever see is an email you choose to send us.

Who we are

Zayvori is made by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, trading as Zayvori. There is no company behind it. That person is the data controller for the little described below, and can be reached at hello@zayvori.com.

The app

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you put in the app — workflows, tasks, notes, due dates and your settings — is stored only on your phone. We never receive it, and we have no server that could.

  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. They are not sent from a server, and nothing about your tasks leaves the device to make them work. Pressing “Mark complete” on a reminder updates the app on your phone, and nowhere else.
  • Notification permission is only used to show those reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app under Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's settings.
  • Android's own backup. Your phone may copy app data to your personal Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is yours: we cannot see or read it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. We keep it only as long as we need to deal with your message and keep a record of it, and we never share it, sell it or add you to a mailing list. Our email provider stores messages on our behalf.

This website

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking, and its fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and nothing of yours on our side. To delete a task or workflow, delete it in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. In practice that means any emails you have sent us. Email hello@zayvori.com and we will respond within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the authority where you live.

Children

Zayvori is not aimed at children, and the app collects nothing from anyone. If a child has emailed us and a parent would like that email deleted, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
