Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

In short: your plans, tasks and notes stay on your phone, and we don’t keep them. The exceptions are the AI features in Zayvori Premium, and only when you use them: Describe it sends the description you type, and Change with AI sends what you type plus the one plan or template you’re changing, to Claude, an AI from Anthropic, to draft the steps or changes. We don’t store any of it. The app has no analytics or tracking; the website counts visits without cookies.

Who we are

Zayvori is made by an independent developer based in the United Kingdom, trading as Zayvori. There is no company behind it. That person is the data controller for the data described below, and can be reached at support@zayvori.com.

The app

There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. Everything you put in the app — plans, tasks, notes, due dates and your settings — is stored only on your phone. The only times any of it leaves your phone are the Premium AI features described below, when you choose to use them.

  • Reminders are scheduled by your phone itself. They are not sent from a server, and nothing about your tasks leaves the device to make them work. Pressing “Mark complete” on a reminder updates the app on your phone, and nowhere else.
  • Microphone permission is only used when you tap the mic in Describe it, to turn what you say into text. Your phone's own speech recognition does this (on Android, Google's speech service, the same one your keyboard's voice typing uses; on recent phones it runs on the device). We never receive the recording, only the text you then choose to send.
  • Calendar permission is only used if you turn on Calendar sync (a Premium feature). Zayvori then adds your tasks’ due dates as events in the calendar you choose on your phone, and updates or removes them as your tasks change. It doesn’t read your other events. The events reach your calendar account (such as Google or Microsoft) through your phone’s own calendar sync, not through us; turning Calendar sync off removes them.
  • Home-screen widget. If you add the Next steps widget, it shows your next steps on your home screen. It reads them from the app on your phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Notification permission is only used to show those reminders. You can turn reminders off in the app under Settings → Reminders, or in your phone's settings.
  • Backups you make in Settings → Your data are files you save wherever you choose, such as Google Drive or email. We never receive them.
  • Templates you send are files you share through your phone, such as by message or email. They hold only the plan (task names, notes, links and relative dates), not your progress, and go only to the people you send them to. We never receive them.
  • Android's own backup. Your phone may copy app data to your personal Google account so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is yours: we cannot see or read it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Describe it

Describe it is optional, and nothing is sent until you choose to use it. The first time, the app tells you what is sent and asks you to continue. When you tap “Draft it”:

  • What is sent: the description you typed or said with the mic, today's date on your phone, your phone's region setting (a country code such as “US”, so holidays like Mother's Day get your country's date), and a random identifier the app makes once when you first use the feature. Nothing else: not your location, your other plans, your name or your contacts.
  • Where it goes: to our service at api.zayvori.com, which runs on Cloudflare, and from there to Anthropic, the maker of Claude, which drafts the steps and sends them back to your phone.
  • What is kept: our service does not store or log your description. Cloudflare, like any network provider, processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the service and keep it secure. Anthropic processes your description as our service provider under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on it; Anthropic may keep it for a limited time for safety and abuse monitoring, as described in its privacy policy.
  • The random identifier is used only to stop the service being overused. It isn't linked to you, your phone's hardware or anything else; uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Why: to provide the feature you asked for. Please don't include things you wouldn't want an AI service to process, such as health details or other people's personal information.

Change with AI works the same way, for a plan or template you already have. When you tap “Suggest changes”, what you type is sent along with that one plan: its task names, notes, due dates and times, the order of its tasks, and which are done. Nothing from your other plans is sent. The first time, the app tells you this and asks you to continue. Nothing changes on your phone until you’ve reviewed the suggestions and tapped Apply.

Cloudflare and Anthropic are based in the United States. Where your description is processed outside the UK or EU, it is protected by the safeguards those companies provide for international transfers, such as standard contractual clauses. See Anthropic's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Zayvori Premium

Describe it, Change with AI and Calendar sync are part of Zayvori Premium, a subscription you buy through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or bank details. To check that a request comes from a subscriber, the app sends the subscription's purchase reference (a code Google Play gives the app) with each Describe it or Change with AI request, and our service asks Google whether it's active. We keep a scrambled form of that reference, never the reference itself: for a few minutes alongside Google’s answer, to avoid asking Google again on every request, and, while you use the AI features, alongside a count of your AI requests, to apply the fair-use allowance in our terms of use (the count starts again each month). We keep nothing else about your purchase. You can manage or cancel the subscription in Google Play at any time.

Emails

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply, on the basis of our legitimate interest in answering you. We keep it only as long as we need to deal with your message and keep a record of it, and we never share it, sell it or add you to a mailing list. Our email provider stores messages on our behalf.

This website

The website counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, to see how people find Zayvori: which pages are visited, which sites or searches brought them, and, as totals, which countries, kinds of device and browsers visitors use. It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser and doesn’t follow you from one visit or site to the next, so there is no cookie banner. Cloudflare receives your IP address to deliver this, as any web service does, and gives us only totals. We use it on the basis of our legitimate interest in knowing how the site is found and which devices it needs to work well on. This is only about the website; the app has no analytics of any kind.

If you arrive from a link with campaign tags (such as utm_source), the site keeps them in its own links as you move between pages (nothing is stored) and passes them to Google Play when you tap a Play link, so we can see which channels lead to installs. Google Play reports these to us only as totals.

The site’s fonts are served from the site itself. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub processes visitors’ IP addresses to deliver the site and keep it secure, as described in GitHub’s privacy statement.

Deleting your data

There is no account to delete, and none of your plans are stored on our side. The only thing we keep is the scrambled purchase reference and request count described above, which can’t be linked back to you. To delete a task or plan, delete it in the app. To delete everything, uninstall the app.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. In practice that means any emails you have sent us; we hold nothing from Describe it or Change with AI. Email support@zayvori.com and we will respond within a month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or the authority where you live.

Children

Zayvori is not aimed at children. If a child has emailed us and a parent would like that email deleted, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
